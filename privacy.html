Privacy Policy

Your photos, your decisions.

This policy covers the ad-free Pikumi iPhone app and these information pages.

Last updated November 17, 2026

1. Who is responsible

Pikumi is developed by Nikita Novikov under the Pikumi name. For privacy questions and requests, use the contact email below.

2. Photos, videos, and iCloud

Pikumi uses the photo library access you grant in iOS to display photos and videos, show media details, and carry out your keep or deletion choices. You can grant access to selected items or your full library, and change or revoke access in iOS Settings.

Pikumi does not upload your photos or videos to the developer. If an item is stored in iCloud, iOS may download it from Apple to display or play it. iCloud Photos and device backups remain subject to your Apple settings and Apple’s privacy policy.

3. Progress stored on your device

Pikumi stores review progress on your device: identifiers of items you have made a decision on, the deletion queue and its media details, review decision history for undo, deletion counts and estimated sizes, and the state used to time App Store rating prompts. This information lets the app resume your review and display statistics. It is not sent to a developer-operated server.

4. No advertising or analytics SDKs

The ad-free edition does not include Google AdMob or its consent SDK. Pikumi has no account registration, developer-operated cloud service, or third-party analytics SDK. The app does not request access to your contacts or precise location.

5. Support messages

If you email support, Nikita Novikov receives your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. They are used to answer your request and resolve the issue. Support messages and their attachments are deleted after the issue is resolved. Please do not send private photos, passwords, or other information that is not needed to explain the problem.

Support mail is received through Gmail (Google). Google processes that mail under its own privacy policy.

6. These web pages

These pages contain no analytics, advertising pixels, contact forms, external fonts, or JavaScript. Their language links do not use cookies or local storage. The hosting service receives the technical information needed to deliver a page, such as your IP address and the requested URL. Its handling of that information is separate from the app’s local photo processing.

7. Retention, deletion, and your choices

You can clear review decisions and the deletion queue using Reset progress; deletion statistics are preserved. Remaining local app data is removed when you delete the app. Removing the app does not delete photos you kept. Device backups can contain app data according to your Apple settings.

Adding an item to the queue does not delete it. Deletion requires confirmation. iOS normally keeps deleted items in Recently Deleted for 30 days; you can manage them in Photos. With iCloud Photos enabled, deletion is synchronized to other devices using the same Apple Account.

8. Privacy requests

You can withdraw photo access in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Pikumi. For access, correction, deletion, or other requests concerning information you sent to support, email the address below. Applicable rights depend on your location; you may also have the right to contact your data protection authority. The developer cannot retrieve your local library or progress from a server.

9. Changes to this policy

If Pikumi’s features or data practices change, this page will be updated. The revision date appears above.
