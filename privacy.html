Pikumi Privacy Policy

This policy explains how information is handled in the ad-free Pikumi app for iPhone and on this website.

Last updated November 17, 2026

1. Developer and contact

Pikumi is developed by Nikita Novikov. For questions about this policy or requests concerning your information, please use the email address in the Contact section.

2. Photo access and iCloud

Pikumi uses the photo access you grant in iOS to show photos, videos, and their details, record your choices, and delete items you confirm. You can allow access to selected items or your entire library, and change or withdraw permission in Settings at any time.

Your photos and videos are processed on your device and are not uploaded to the developer. For items stored in iCloud, iOS may download a copy from Apple to display or play them. iCloud Photos and device backups are managed by Apple according to your settings and Apple’s privacy policy.

3. Information saved on your device

Pikumi saves your review progress so you can pick up where you left off. This includes identifiers for reviewed items, details of items in the deletion queue, and a history of your choices so you can undo them.

The app also saves deletion totals, estimated media sizes, and information used to decide when to show an App Store rating prompt. This information stays in local app storage; the developer does not receive it. Apple-managed backups may include app data, depending on your settings.

4. Advertising, analytics, and accounts

This edition has no advertising or third-party usage analytics. It does not require an account and has no developer-operated server or cloud service.

5. Support emails

When you contact support, Nikita Novikov receives your email address, message, and any attachments you send. This information is used to reply and help resolve your request. The developer deletes the correspondence and attachments after the request is resolved.

Support uses Gmail. Google processes emails under its own privacy policy, including its rules on retention and backups. Deleting correspondence from the developer’s mailbox does not mean every copy held by Google is immediately erased. Please send only what is needed to explain the issue and avoid private photos or passwords.

6. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub receives your IP address and technical information about your page requests to deliver the website. GitHub’s privacy statement explains how it handles that information.

GitHub also records and retains visitor IP addresses for security purposes.

The pages contain no analytics, advertising trackers, contact forms, external fonts, or JavaScript. Changing the language does not save your choice in cookies or browser storage.

7. Resetting progress and deleting data

Reset progress clears your review choices and deletion queue while keeping deletion statistics. Deleting Pikumi removes its local app data from your device. It does not delete the photos and videos you kept or remove data from backups managed by Apple.

Adding an item to the queue does not delete it. Deletion requires your confirmation. Deleted items can usually be recovered in the Photos app’s Recently Deleted album for 30 days, unless you permanently delete them sooner. If iCloud Photos is enabled, deletion also syncs to other devices using the same Apple Account.

8. Your choices and privacy requests

You can change or withdraw photo access in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Pikumi. The developer does not hold a copy of your local library or review progress and cannot retrieve it for you.

For requests to access, correct, or delete information you sent to support, please use the contact email below. Your rights depend on applicable law and may include the right to complain to a data protection authority.

9. Policy updates

This policy will be updated if Pikumi’s features or information handling change. The latest revision date is shown at the top of the page.
