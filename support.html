Frequently asked questions

What do I need to use Pikumi?

You need an iPhone running iOS 26 or later and permission to access the photos and videos you want to review. Pikumi has no advertising.

Does swiping delete an item straight away?

No. Swipe right or tap Keep to keep an item. Swipe left or tap Queue to add it to the deletion queue. When you are ready, open the queue, review its contents, and confirm deletion.

Can I undo a choice?

The Undo button reverses your last review choice. You can also remove items from the queue before confirming deletion. Once an item has been deleted, use Recently Deleted in Photos to recover it while it is still available there. Pikumi’s Undo button cannot restore deleted items.

Why are some photos missing?

Check Pikumi’s photo permission and the media type and date filters currently in use. With access to selected photos, Pikumi can only show the items you have allowed it to access.

Why is a photo or video not loading?

An item stored in iCloud may need an internet connection to download. Check your connection and whether the item opens in Photos, then try again in Pikumi. Also check that Pikumi still has permission to access it.

Why has my free storage not increased?

Deleted items usually remain in Recently Deleted for 30 days. Pikumi’s size figures estimate the size of the media; they do not measure the space immediately freed on your iPhone. A size estimate may not be available for every item.

Will deletion affect iCloud and my other devices?

Yes, if iCloud Photos is enabled. Deletions sync to other devices using the same Apple Account. Check the queue before confirming deletion.

How can I change photo access?

Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Pikumi. You can change which photos the app can access or withdraw access.

Does the developer receive my photos?

Pikumi does not send your photo library to the developer. iOS may download iCloud items from Apple for viewing. If you attach a photo to a support email, the developer receives it with your message. The Privacy Policy explains these separate uses.
