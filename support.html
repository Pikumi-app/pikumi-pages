Frequently asked questions

What do I need to use Pikumi?

An iPhone running iOS 26 or later and permission to access the photos and videos you want to review. This edition has no advertising.

Are items deleted as soon as I swipe?

No. Swipe right or tap Keep to keep an item. Swipe left or tap Queue to add it to the deletion queue. Open the queue, check the items, and confirm deletion when you are ready.

Can I undo a choice?

Use Undo to reverse the last review decision, or remove an item from the queue before confirming deletion. After deletion is confirmed, recovery is managed in the Photos app’s Recently Deleted album, not through Pikumi’s Undo button.

Why are some photos missing?

Pikumi can only show items that iOS makes available to it. Check the app’s photo access and the active media and date filters. With limited access, only the items you selected for Pikumi are available.

Why will a photo or video not load?

An iCloud item may need an internet connection to download. Check your connection, confirm the item opens in Photos, and then open it in Pikumi again. Also check that Pikumi still has access to that item.

Why did available storage not increase immediately?

iOS normally retains deleted items in Recently Deleted for 30 days. Pikumi’s displayed sizes are estimates of media size, not a measurement of instantly recovered storage. Some items have no available size estimate.

Does deletion affect iCloud and my other devices?

Yes. When iCloud Photos is enabled, confirmed deletions are synchronized to other devices using the same Apple Account. Check the queue carefully before confirming.

How do I change photo access?

Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Pikumi and choose the access you want to allow.

Are my photos sent to the developer?

No. Pikumi processes your library on your device. Apple may download iCloud items for display or playback. The Privacy Policy explains local progress, Apple services, and support messages.
